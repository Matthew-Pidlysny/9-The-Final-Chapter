## ✅ Complete System Features

### 1. **Advanced Linguistic Analysis**
- **4 Pattern Detection Systems:**
  - Business Discretion Detection (hidden meetings, sudden changes, unverifiable claims)
  - Altruistic Conspiracy Analysis (sudden philanthropy, metaphysical endorsements, timing suspicions)
  - Sovereignty Pattern Detection (legal immunity, elite status, religious authority)
  - Deception Indicators (vague language, misdirection, contradictions)

### 2. **Evidence Scoring System**
- **0.0 to 1.0 Scale:** Quantifies strength of detected patterns
- **Weighted Combination:** Different pattern types have different weights
- **Organization Boost:** Mentions of tracked organizations increase evidence score
- **Minimum Threshold:** Only articles with 60%+ evidence are published

- **1-5 Skulls (💀):** Visual indicator of article significance
- **Based on Evidence + Patterns:** Higher evidence and more patterns = more skulls

Articles must meet ALL criteria to be published:
